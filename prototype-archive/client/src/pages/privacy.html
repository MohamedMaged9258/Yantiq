Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026 · Version 1.0

Yantiq is designed for children. We take child privacy extremely seriously and comply with COPPA (US), GDPR (EU), and applicable child data protection laws.

1. Who We Are

Yantiq ("we", "our", "us") is an educational application that teaches Arabic reading to children ages 3–8. Parent or guardian accounts are required to create child profiles. We never allow children to register directly.

2. Data We Collect

Parent/Guardian Data

  • Email address and full name (required for account)
  • Hashed password (never stored in plaintext)
  • Login timestamps and IP address (security logging)

Child Data (minimal, necessary only)

  • First name and nickname (display purposes)
  • Age (not date of birth — for curriculum placement)
  • Avatar emoji choice
  • Learning progress, stars, badges, and session history
  • Audio recordings during pronunciation practice (processed then discarded)

❌ What we do NOT collect

  • Date of birth, address, or phone number
  • Device location or GPS data
  • Photos or video of the child
  • Social media accounts or contacts
  • Behavioural advertising data

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide and personalise the Arabic learning experience
  • To generate pronunciation scores using the Anthropic Claude AI API
  • To send progress reports and notifications (with your consent)
  • To detect and prevent fraud and abuse
  • We never sell data to advertisers or data brokers
  • We never share child data with third parties except for core service providers (AI API, email service)

4. Audio Recording Policy

The microphone is activated only when your child taps the "Speak" button. Recordings are sent to the Anthropic Claude API to generate a pronunciation score, then permanently deleted. Audio is stored temporarily (max 90 days) in encrypted cloud storage solely for debugging and quality improvement.

You can request deletion of all audio recordings at any time by contacting privacy@yantiq.app.

5. Parental Rights (COPPA & GDPR)

  • Access: Request a copy of all data we hold for your child
  • Correction: Update any inaccurate child data in Settings
  • Deletion: Delete your account to permanently remove all data (cascade)
  • Portability: Request a machine-readable export of your data
  • Withdrawal: Withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawfulness

To exercise these rights, email privacy@yantiq.app. We respond within 30 days as required by law.

6. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt (cost factor 12). All data is transmitted over TLS 1.2+. Authentication uses short-lived JWT tokens (15 minutes) with secure HttpOnly refresh cookies. Child data is isolated by parent account — no cross-family data access is possible.

7. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained while account is active
  • Audio recordings: deleted after 90 days automatically
  • Session logs: retained for 2 years for learning analytics
  • All data: permanently deleted within 30 days of account deletion

8. Cookies

We use a single secure HttpOnly cookie for authentication (refresh token). We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will notify you by email at least 14 days before material changes to this policy. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
