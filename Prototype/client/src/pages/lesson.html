💡 About Tashkeel (تشكيل)

The small marks above/below letters ( َ ِ ُ ) are short vowels. They're essential for early reading — every letter shows them so your child learns correct pronunciation from day one!

بَ Fatha "a"
بِ Kasra "i"
بُ Damma "u"
